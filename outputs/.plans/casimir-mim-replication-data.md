# Research Plan: Casimir-cavity MIM replication data landscape

## Objective
Determine whether independent experimental data already exists that could feed or pre-empt a planned replication of Moddel-group Casimir-cavity MIM devices (Phys. Rev. Research 3, L022007 (2021); Symmetry 13(3), 517 (2021)).

## Questions
1. Have any groups OTHER than Moddel's attempted replication of Casimir-cavity-induced conductance changes or zero-bias currents in MIM devices? (published, preprint, thesis, conference)
2. Are there publicly available RAW DATASETS (Zenodo, OSF, Figshare, Dryad, arXiv ancillary, journal supplements, university repos) with I-V curves, zero-bias current time series, or cavity-open-vs-closed measurements?
3. Do PhD/MS theses from CU Boulder (Weerakkody, Doroski, Bartusiak, Dee) or elsewhere contain device data beyond the two 2021 papers?
4. Has anyone published artifact attribution or critique with MEASUREMENTS (not just theory) — e.g. thermoelectric or Pd-hydride explanations tested experimentally?
5. What follow-on Moddel-group papers exist 2022–2026 with new device data? Does the Quantum Reports 2024 modeling paper carry data?
6. Do patents exist with experimental data tables for these devices?

## Strategy
- 5 parallel researchers, each covering a disjoint dimension
- R1: Academic literature search (papers, preprints, conference proceedings) for independent replications and critiques
- R2: Data repository search (Zenodo, OSF, Figshare, Dryad, arXiv ancillary, journal supplements)
- R3: Thesis search (ProQuest, CU Boulder IR, ETD databases) + Moddel group member tracking
- R4: Moddel-group follow-on work 2022-2026 + patent search
- R5: Artifact/critique literature with experimental measurements + Casimir-cavity theory community response
- Expected rounds: 1-2

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] All 6 questions answered with evidence or verified absence
- [ ] Each claimed independent replication cross-verified
- [ ] Each dataset URL directly checked for accessibility
- [ ] No single-source claims on critical findings
- [ ] Contradictions identified and addressed
