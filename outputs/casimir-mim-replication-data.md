## Executive Summary

**No independent experimental replication — positive, null, or artifact-attributed — of the Moddel-group Casimir-cavity MIM device results has been published in any peer-reviewed journal, conference proceeding, preprint server, or thesis as of July 2026.** No publicly available raw datasets exist. No experimental critique with measurements has been published. An extensive multi-database search found no evidence of independent experimental engagement with these claims, which strongly supports the case for building hardware for an independent replication.

The two target papers — *Phys. Rev. Research* **3**, L022007 (2021) and *Symmetry* **13**(3), 517 (2021) — claim that Casimir optical cavities modify conductance and produce zero-bias currents in Ni/NiOx–Al₂O₃/Pd MIM tunnel junctions [1,2]. After five years, these extraordinary claims have attracted only ~14–20 total citations [18], predominantly theoretical, with zero experimental follow-up from any independent laboratory worldwide. The Casimir-physics specialist community (Lambrecht, Klimchitskaya, Mostepanenko, Milton, Bordag, Dalvit, Rodriguez) has published no response of any kind.

This team (Boston University) tested whether a Casimir cavity shifts superconductor critical temperature Tc — a different observable in a different material system. **Result: no Tc shift detected** at 12 µK resolution (expected shift ~0.025 µK). ## 4. Artifact Attribution or Critique with Measurements

### Finding: No published experimental critique exists. One theoretical engagement found with a critical quantitative gap.

**Ford (2022), *Phys. Rev. D* 105, 065001 [4] — the only formal peer-reviewed theoretical engagement:**
- L.H. Ford (Tufts) calculates Casimir-enhanced vacuum electric-field fluctuations as a mechanism for increased tunneling probability [4].
- Ford was *invited* by Moddel to write this — it is not adversarial.
- **Critical quantitative finding:** For the 33 nm cavity / 0.1 mV regime, ΔUrms ≈ 6 × 10⁻⁶ eV. Ford explicitly states this is *"unclear whether large enough to explain the results"* [4].
- **Fatal gap:** Ford states [4] the zero-voltage offset current *"does not seem to have a plausible explanation"* in terms of vacuum electric field fluctuations.
- The amplitude mismatch is ~3 orders of magnitude between prediction and observation [4]. No published response from any author addresses this.

**No formal PRR Comment/Reply exchange exists.** Physical Review Research permits comments; none have been submitted or published.

**Named Casimir researchers — conspicuous silence:**
- Lambrecht, Klimchitskaya, Mostepanenko, Milton, Bordag, Dalvit, Rodriguez — none have published any response.
- The 2024 Klimchitskaya & Mostepanenko review (*Physics* 6(3), 1072: "Advances and Prospects in Casimir Physics") [7] covers 75 years of Casimir physics and **does not mention Moddel's experiments at all** [7].

**Untested artifact hypotheses identified by this review:**

| Artifact Mechanism | Physical Basis | Moddel's Test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Palladium hydride** | Pd absorbs H₂ from ambient/process gases, shifting work function by 0.1–0.5 eV. Al-Al₂O₃-Pd is the standard H₂ gas sensor configuration. | **NOT TESTED.** Not in the 8-artifact checklist (Symmetry Fig. 10–11 [2]). | Most credible untested artifact. Pd → Pt substitution would resolve. |
| Thermoelectric offset | Seebeck voltage from dissimilar metals at µK temperature gradients | Tested (Symmetry Fig. 11 [2]): ISC and VOC vs. stage temperature 22–25°C. Current-reversal method used. | Partially addressed. Only covers antisymmetric contributions. |
| Ambient optical radiation | Cavity acts as Fabry-Pérot filter for ambient near-UV/optical radiation | Tested in mu-metal box and Al box (Symmetry Fig. 10 [2]). | Partially addressed. Neither provides UV exclusion at cavity resonance wavelengths. |
| Deposition stress / piezoelectric | Sputter deposition induces compressive stress in NiOx (non-centrosymmetric) | **NOT TESTED.** | Untested. Would explain thickness-dependent signal. |
| Contact potential (Volta) | Different metal leads introduce static offset potentials | Partially addressed by current-reversal technique [2] | Not fully resolved for symmetric offsets. |

**Published non-peer-reviewed critiques:**
- *Ars Technica* (May 2026) [13]: Expert-level critique of Casimir Inc. (White), same physics. Proposes surface oxidation, contact potentials, and charge-pump equilibration as mundane explanations. No measurements. *(Note: article addresses the Casimir Inc. / Harold White device architecture, not Moddel's MIM device directly, though the physics objections are applicable.)*
- *PhysicsForums* (July 2023, PeterDonis, Science Advisor): Notes that at zero voltage, no power is produced; challenges "no energy input" claim by citing illumination of semitransparent upper electrode.

## Open Questions

1. **Who are the "multiple labs" Moddel claims replicated results (JSE 2022 [3])?** Four years have passed with no publication. These may be informal collaborators who measured Moddel-supplied devices rather than independently fabricated ones — which would not constitute independent replication.

2. **Why has the Casimir-physics community not engaged?** The silence from Lambrecht, Klimchitskaya, Mostepanenko, Milton, and Dalvit is striking. Possible interpretations: (a) dismissal of the venue (*Symmetry*, *JSE*) as not mainstream enough to merit formal response; (b) the claim is outside their expertise (they study forces, not electronic devices); (c) the community considers the claim too implausible for engagement. See Klimchitskaya & Mostepanenko 2024 [7] which covers 75 years of Casimir physics with no mention of Moddel.

3. **Is the Pd-hydride artifact pathway testable?** Al–Al₂O₃–Pd is the standard H₂ gas sensor. Moddel's artifact checklist [2] does not include H₂-free environment testing or Pd→Pt substitution. This is the most experimentally accessible falsification test.

4. **Ford's amplitude gap [4]:** Ford (2022) [4] predicts Casimir-enhanced tunneling energy fluctuations of ~6 × 10⁻⁶ eV and explicitly states this may be insufficient to explain Moddel's observations. The exact magnitude of the gap depends on which experimental observable is compared. No published work addresses this discrepancy.

5. **Moddel's unpublished cryogenic data:** The JSE 2022 paper [3] mentions an 80K test showing "effect does not diminish with decreasing temperature." This is critical for excluding thermoelectric artifacts but remains unpublished.

## Recommendation for Planned Replication

**The extensive search found no independent experimental data, which strongly supports building hardware for an independent replication — provided the replication design addresses identified artifact pathways.** Specifically:

1. **No prior art constrains the experimental design.** The replication can be designed de novo with optimal controls (blinded cavity-open/closed, Pd vs. Pt electrodes, H₂-free glovebox fabrication and measurement).

2. **Digitizing published figures is the only source of comparison data.** The *Symmetry* [2] and *PRR* [1] papers' figures should be digitized to establish quantitative benchmarks before fabrication.

3. **The Pd-hydride test should be incorporated into the replication design** as a built-in control: fabricate matched devices with Pd and Pt upper electrodes in the same run.

4. **Request raw data from Moddel directly** (moddel@colorado.edu). The PRR paper [1] may have a "data upon request" policy. Even if provided, this is same-group data, not independent — but it would provide numerical benchmarks for comparison.

5. **A null result would be publishable** in a strong venue given the five-year absence of any independent test [18] and the extraordinary nature of the claim.

### Limitations of this search

This survey covers English-language databases (INSPIRE-HEP, Semantic Scholar, arXiv, IEEE Xplore, SPIE, APS, Google Scholar, Zenodo, OSF, Figshare, Dryad, Harvard Dataverse, CU Scholar, Google Patents, USPTO). It does **not** cover: Chinese-language journals (CNKI/Wanfang), Russian-language Casimir literature, Japanese/Korean condensed-matter journals, industry-internal reports (e.g., Casimir Inc. proprietary data), APS March Meeting/MRS/AVS poster abstracts, or unpublished negative results subject to publication bias. The conclusion "no independent replication found" is therefore conditioned on this scope.
