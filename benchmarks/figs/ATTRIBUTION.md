`../benchmarks.json` can be independently re-verified against their sources:

  authors, CC BY 4.0 (MDPI).
- `prr_*.png`: Moddel, Weerakkody, Doroski, Bartusiak, "Casimir-cavity-induced
  conductance changes," *Physical Review Research* 3, L022007 (2021),

Supplemental Material, CC BY 4.0, preserved as the verification source for the
