# zpe-lab

Governed experimental research program: zero-point / vacuum-energy extraction hypothesis.
Telos: design, build, instrument, and falsify the simplest prototype capable of demonstrating net usable energy from vacuum phenomena — or rigorously falsify the hypothesis in the tested design space.

Governance artifacts (intents, research, pressure-tests, promotions) live in the meanmode vault under vault/*/zpe-lab/.
Engineering artifacts (models, simulations, schematics, instrument configs, raw measurement data) live HERE.

Honesty invariants (from telos):
- Never claim success unless net energy survives full input/loss/uncertainty accounting, blinded controls, and independent replication.
- Heat, vibration, EMI, grounding, stored charge, calibration error, and hidden external sources are ALWAYS competing explanations to be excluded.
- Negative results are first-class deliverables. Never redefine failure as success.
