## Title
**First independent test of the Casimir-cavity MIM "vacuum energy" claim — a pre-registered,

## The claim, and the hole in the record (150 words)
In 2021, an NSF-funded group at CU Boulder published, in *Physical Review Research*, the
extraordinary claim that metal-insulator-metal tunnel junctions faced with sub-micron optical
("Casimir") cavities produce zero-bias currents drawn from vacuum fluctuations — tens of
nanoamps, patent-protected (US11133758B2 family), with a stated power density of 70 W/m² in a
companion paper. Five years later, the verified state of the record is: **zero independent
replications** (positive, null, or artifact-attributed), zero public raw datasets, zero
experimental critiques, and the specialist Casimir community's complete silence. The authors'
own Supplemental Material reports a persistent ~6 µV anomalous offset they "have thus far been
unable to eliminate," with the promised follow-up publication never appearing. An
extraordinary claim of this magnitude — either revolutionary physics or an instructive,
publishable artifact — should not sit untested for five years because nobody bothered.

## What I will do (200 words)
A fabrication-independent, blinded replication built to discriminate — not merely to repeat:

- **Devices fabricated at an independent facility** (quotes in progress at RIT SMFL and
  Texas A&M AggieFab), replicating the published stack, PLUS controls the original work never
  ran: matched Pd/Pt/Ni top electrodes in one run (the palladium-hydride artifact pathway —
  Pd/Al₂O₃ is literally the standard hydrogen-sensor configuration — is absent from the
  original artifact checklist) and hydrogen-controlled handling.
- **A same-junction cavity-open/closed protocol** via a retractable mirror — eliminating the
  device-to-device variability that dominates the published between-device comparisons.
- **Pre-registered, frozen analysis**: the full pipeline (blinded run labels held by a
  separate party, sealed instrument-constant records, interleaved "decoy" actuation controls
  that separate drive-correlated artifacts from mirror-correlated physics, and a 3σ criterion
  over a quantified seven-class artifact budget) is committed and adversarially reviewed
  BEFORE any device exists. Changing the analysis after data exists is structurally visible.
- **Publication in any outcome**: confirmation (extraordinary), artifact attribution (names
  the mundane source — the most likely and most useful outcome), or a clean bounded null.

## Why fund an independent, unaffiliated researcher (100 words)
Because that is the point: every positive result traces to one group; the first independent
test must come from outside it, fabricated at a facility with no tie to the original
institution. The project's rigor is documented rather than asserted — every protocol,
artifact budget, and analysis decision has survived multiple rounds of adversarial review and
is committed to a public-ready repository with its full revision history, including the
reviews that failed and the corrections they forced. The pre-registration is auditable today,
before a dollar of fabrication is spent.
